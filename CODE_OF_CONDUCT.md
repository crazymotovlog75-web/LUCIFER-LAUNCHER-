# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all users of Zyron Launcher, regardless of their background.

## Our Standards
* Respect other community members and testers.
* Do not distribute modified or cracked APKs of Zyron Launcher.
* Report bugs constructively to the owner, **Saif Khan**.

## Enforcement
The creator, **Saif Khan**, reserves all rights to ban or restrict users from official community spaces who violate these terms.
